## Variants (all seamless loops, exported as WAV master + MP3 192 kbps + OGG Vorbis)

| File | Length | Source region | Thunder handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rain-loop.*` | 0:60 | 3:55–4:59 | none present (calm steady section) |
| `rain-loop-long.*` | 11:03 | 2:55–14:10 | **spliced** — clap cut out, crossfaded across; rain untouched |
| `rain-loop-long-hipass.*` | 11:11 | 2:55–14:10 (uncut) | **300 Hz high-pass** — clap suppressed in place; rain slightly thinner |

**Modifications (required disclosure under CC-BY):** downloaded the original
18-minute recording, extracted the steady-rain middle (skipping the quiet
intro and the fade-out outro), removed the single thunderclap at ~9:56 (by
splice in one variant, by high-pass filter in the other), and built seamless
loops by equal-power crossfading the tail back over the head. Re-encoded to
MP3 and OGG.
